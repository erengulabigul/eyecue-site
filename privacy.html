Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

In short: Eyecue doesn't collect, store or share any personal data on our servers — we don't have any. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your scripts and recordings stay on your device.

This policy explains how the Eyecue app ("Eyecue", "the app", "we") handles your information. Eyecue is developed by Eren Can Gülabigül.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no sign-up, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not send your content to us or to any third party.

Content you create

  • Scripts you write or import are stored only on your device.
  • Recordings (video and audio) are stored only on your device, inside the app. If you turn on “Save Videos to Photos”, videos are also copied to your photo library.
  • Like other app data, this content may be included in your device backups (for example, iCloud Backup) according to your own device settings. These backups are managed by Apple, not by us.

Camera, microphone and photos

Eyecue asks for access to the camera and microphone only to record your videos and audio, and for permission to add to your photo library only to save your videos there. Eyecue cannot read your existing photos. Nothing is uploaded.

Speech recognition (Voice Follow)

Voice Follow uses Apple's speech recognition to scroll the script as you speak. On devices that support it, speech is processed entirely on your device. On devices that don't, Apple may process the audio on its servers to provide the transcription; that processing is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive your audio or transcripts. Voice Follow only listens while you use it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment information. The app only checks with Apple whether your subscription is active.

Children

Eyecue is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can delete any script or recording in the app at any time, and you can change camera, microphone, photos and speech recognition permissions in your device's Settings. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
